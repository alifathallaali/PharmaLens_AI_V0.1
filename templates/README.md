# PharmaLens AI — Module 15
## Market Access & Institutional Intelligence

This module connects commercial market data to institution-level action:
**Market → Institution → Department → Stakeholder → Formulary → Procurement/Tender → Opportunity → KAM Action → Target → Revenue**

### Included
- `market_access.py` — reusable scoring/analytics engines
- `15_market_access_institutional_intelligence.ipynb` — demonstration notebook
- CSV templates for institutions, formulary/listing, tenders, and KAM/stakeholders

### Core engines
1. Institution prioritization and hospital segmentation
2. Formulary/listing opportunity scoring
3. Tender opportunity scoring
4. Account share and untapped opportunity
5. Stakeholder priority scoring
6. Contract renewal probability
7. Price/access trade-off
8. Access-barrier classification
9. KAM account-plan structure

### Integration
Designed to feed PharmaLens dashboards, Recommendations, AI Copilot, Target Planning, SFE, GTM, Market Access, Hospital/Institutional Business, and reporting.

### Data note
All rows in the templates are synthetic examples. Replace them only with licensed, authorized, or user-owned data before production use.
