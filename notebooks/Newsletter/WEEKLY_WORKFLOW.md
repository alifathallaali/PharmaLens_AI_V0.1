# 2-Hour Weekly Production Workflow

## Monday — Research (30 min)
Collect 10–20 developments and primary sources.

## Tuesday — Selection (20 min)
Score each item:
Relevance 1–5
Impact 1–5
Novelty 1–5
Commercial relevance 1–5
Evidence quality 1–5

Pick the top 5–7.

## Wednesday — Analysis (30 min)
Build:
- signal
- numbers
- competitive map
- commercial implication
- watchlist

## Thursday — Draft (25 min)
Use the weekly template.

## Friday — Publish & Repurpose (15 min)
Publish Substack.
Create:
- 1 LinkedIn carousel
- 1 chart
- 1 short post
- 1 short-form video script

## Rule
AI accelerates research and drafting. Human review remains mandatory for factual accuracy and interpretation.
