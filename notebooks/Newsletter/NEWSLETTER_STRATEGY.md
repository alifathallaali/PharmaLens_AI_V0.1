# PharmaLens Intelligence — Editorial Strategy

## 1. Position
**Global pharma intelligence + MENA commercial intelligence + AI-powered analysis.**

Primary audience:
- Brand/Product Managers
- Commercial Excellence / SFE
- Sales Managers
- Business Development
- Market Access
- Medical Affairs
- Pharma executives
- Pharma/biotech analysts and investors

Secondary audience:
- Consultants
- Healthcare founders
- Pharma students/professionals

## 2. Editorial pillars
1. Pharma in 60 Seconds — major developments
2. Money & Markets — revenue, deals, valuations, R&D, patent cliffs
3. Pipeline & Innovation — molecules, trials, modalities, AI drug discovery
4. Commercial Intelligence — market share, launches, GTM, pricing, forecasting
5. MENA Pharma — Saudi, Egypt, UAE, GCC
6. AI × Pharma — AI applications across R&D and commercial functions
7. Market Access — payer, hospital, formulary, tender and reimbursement intelligence
8. Medical Affairs — KOLs, publications, evidence and trials

## 3. Signature format
### THE SNAPSHOT
3–7 most important developments.

### THE SIGNAL
What changed and why it matters.

### THE NUMBERS
A small table/chart with 2–6 decision-relevant metrics.

### THE COMPETITIVE MAP
Who benefits, who is threatened, and what to watch.

### PHARMALENS VIEW
A clearly labeled interpretation. Never present interpretation as fact.

### COMMERCIAL IMPLICATION
What Brand, Sales, Market Access, Medical and BD teams should consider.

### WATCHLIST
3–5 things to monitor next.

### SOURCES
Primary/authoritative sources first. Include publication dates and links.

## 4. Voice
- Clear, intelligent, concise
- Evidence-led
- Commercially useful
- No clickbait
- Explain technical terms in plain language
- Distinguish fact, inference and opinion

## 5. Free vs future paid
FREE:
- Weekly flagship issue
- Major pharma news
- Selected charts
- MENA highlights
- Short analyses

FUTURE PREMIUM:
- Deep-dive reports
- Full competitive landscapes
- Interactive datasets
- Forecasts
- Market access intelligence
- Company/brand trackers
- PharmaLens AI templates and decision tools

Do not paywall the core audience-building content too early.
