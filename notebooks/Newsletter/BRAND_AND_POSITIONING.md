# Brand & Positioning

## Recommended publication name
**PharmaLens Intelligence**

Alternative names:
- PharmaLens Brief
- PharmaLens Signal
- PharmaLens Pharma Intelligence
- PharmaLens Weekly

Recommended tagline:
**See the signal. Understand the market. Make the decision.**

Alternative:
**Pharma intelligence, distilled for decision-makers.**

## Visual direction
- Clean editorial layout
- Strong typography
- Data-first charts
- One dominant insight per visual
- Consistent source footer
- Avoid generic stock-photo pharma imagery

## Content labels
FACT — verified information
DATA — quantitative evidence
PHARMALENS VIEW — interpretation
WATCH — future indicator
MENA SIGNAL — regional insight
AI × PHARMA — technology insight
