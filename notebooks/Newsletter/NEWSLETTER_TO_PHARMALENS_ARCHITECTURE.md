  ↓
Short insight / chart / video
  ↓
Substack
  ↓
Weekly analysis

## Intelligence layer
Sources
  ↓
Evidence + source registry
  ↓
AI classification
  ↓
Topic/entity/company/therapy extraction
  ↓
Signal scoring
  ↓
Editorial review
  ↓
Newsletter

## Product layer
Newsletter CTA
  ↓
PharmaLens landing page
  ↓
Free interactive insight
  ↓
Account
  ↓
Limited credits / trial
  ↓
Paid PharmaLens

## Content reuse loop
One research package can become:
1 weekly Substack issue
3 LinkedIn posts
1 carousel
1 short Arabic reel
1 English short video
1 chart
1 PharmaLens interactive analysis
1 email intelligence brief

## UGC/referral idea
Readers can share a “PharmaLens Signal Card”:
- Signal
- 1 chart
- 1 sentence interpretation
- Source
- “Analyze this in PharmaLens”

Reward later with credits/features, subject to platform/payment rules.
